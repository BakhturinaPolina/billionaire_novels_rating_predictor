# Stage 07: Topic Quality Analysis - Research Report

## Executive Summary

This report documents the methods and results of the topic quality analysis performed on the retrained BERTopic model. The analysis identifies candidate noisy topics through exploratory data analysis (EDA) using topic size, part-of-speech (POS) representation statistics, and semantic coherence metrics. This stage serves as a quality control checkpoint before proceeding to LLM-generated topic labeling.

**Key Findings:**
- **Total topics analyzed**: 368 (excluding outlier topic -1)
- **Candidate noisy topics identified**: 13 (3.5% of all topics)
- **Topics with POS words < 10**: 20 (5.4% of all topics)
- **Primary noise indicators**: Few POS words (<3) and low coherence scores

---

## 1. Methods

### 1.1 Overview

The topic quality analysis is a non-destructive EDA process that evaluates topics across three dimensions:
1. **Topic size**: Number of documents assigned to each topic
2. **POS representation**: Quality and quantity of part-of-speech words in topic representations
3. **Semantic coherence**: Per-topic coherence scores using the c_v metric

The analysis flags topics for manual inspection but does not remove or modify the underlying topic model structure.

### 1.2 Data Sources

**Model Input:**
- **Model**: Retrained BERTopic model (Pareto rank 1)
- **Embedding model**: `paraphrase-MiniLM-L6-v2`
- **Model format**: Pickle wrapper containing `RetrainableBERTopicModel` with embedded training dataset
- **Model location**: `models/retrained/paraphrase-MiniLM-L6-v2/model_1.pkl`

**Corpus Data:**
- **Training dataset**: 680,822 documents (from wrapper's `dataset_as_list_of_strings`)
- **Dictionary corpus**: OCTIS corpus TSV (`data/interim/octis/corpus.tsv`)
- **Dictionary size**: 185,903 unique tokens

**Configuration Parameters:**
- `min_topic_size`: 30 documents (minimum threshold for valid topics)
- `min_pos_words`: 3 words (minimum POS words per topic)
- `min_pos_coherence`: 0.0 (minimum coherence threshold)
- `top_k`: 10 (number of top words per topic considered)

### 1.3 Methodology

#### 1.3.1 Topic Distribution Analysis

**Function**: `get_topic_distribution()`

Extracts basic topic statistics from the BERTopic model:
- Topic ID
- Document count per topic
- Topic name and representation
- Size-based validity flag (`keep_by_size`)

Topics with fewer than 30 documents are flagged as potentially noisy.

#### 1.3.2 POS Representation Statistics

**Function**: `get_pos_representation_stats()`

Analyzes the part-of-speech (POS) representation aspect of topics:
- Extracts POS representation words for each topic (top 10 words)
- Counts the number of valid POS words per topic
- Identifies topics with insufficient POS word coverage

The POS representation is one of multiple topic aspects generated by BERTopic, specifically designed to capture semantically meaningful nouns, verbs, and adjectives that characterize the topic.

#### 1.3.3 Per-Topic Coherence Computation

**Function**: `compute_pos_coherence_per_topic()`

Computes semantic coherence scores for each topic using the c_v coherence metric from Gensim:

1. **Word-to-ID mapping**: Converts POS representation words to dictionary token IDs
2. **Coherence model**: Uses `CoherenceModel` with:
   - `coherence="c_v"`: Confirms the c_v coherence measure
   - `texts=docs_tokens`: Tokenized document corpus
   - `dictionary=dictionary`: Gensim dictionary built from OCTIS corpus
3. **Per-topic scores**: Computes coherence for each topic's POS word set

**Coherence Metric (c_v):**
The c_v coherence measure evaluates topic quality by measuring the degree of semantic similarity between words in a topic. It uses:
- **Segmentation**: Sliding window approach over the corpus
- **Probability estimation**: Word co-occurrence probabilities
- **Score range**: Typically 0.0 to 1.0 (higher = more coherent)

The computation uses parallel processing (11 processes) with batch processing (64 documents per batch) for efficiency.

#### 1.3.4 Noise Candidate Detection

**Function**: `build_topic_quality_table()`

Combines all metrics and applies flagging logic:

**Flagging Conditions:**
1. `flag_small`: Topic has fewer than `min_topic_size` documents
2. `flag_few_pos`: Topic has fewer than `min_pos_words` POS words
3. `flag_low_coh`: Topic has coherence score below `min_pos_coherence`

**Noise Candidate Classification:**
A topic is flagged as a `noise_candidate` if it meets any of the flagging conditions. The `noise_reason` field contains a semicolon-separated list of all applicable reasons.

**Inspection Labels:**
Human-readable labels are generated for manual review:
- Format: `[NOISE_CANDIDATE:<reasons>] <topic_name>`
- Example: `[NOISE_CANDIDATE:few_pos<3;low_coh<0.00] 17____`

### 1.4 Implementation Details

**Code Structure:**
- **Main script**: `src/stage07_topic_quality/main.py`
- **Analysis functions**: `src/stage07_topic_quality/topic_quality_analysis.py`
- **Notebook**: `notebooks/06_labeling/06_topic_quality_eda.ipynb`

**Processing Pipeline:**
1. Load retrained BERTopic model (wrapper or native format)
2. Load documents and tokenize (batched processing, 50,000 documents per batch)
3. Build or load Gensim dictionary from OCTIS corpus
4. Extract topic distributions
5. Extract POS representation statistics
6. Compute per-topic coherence scores (parallel processing)
7. Combine metrics and flag noise candidates
8. Generate inspection labels
9. Save results to CSV files

**Performance:**
- Dictionary building: ~45 seconds (680,822 documents)
- Coherence computation: ~54 seconds (parallel processing, 11 processes)
- Total analysis time: ~55 seconds

## 3. Discussion

### 3.1 Noise Candidate Characteristics

The identified noise candidates exhibit several patterns:

1. **Empty or near-empty representations**: Topics 17, 18, 132, 186, 224 have empty word lists, suggesting they capture uninterpretable or noise content.

2. **Single-word topics**: Topics with only 1-2 POS words (141, 183, 241, 262, 347) lack semantic richness and may represent:
   - Artifacts of the clustering process
   - Rare or edge-case patterns
   - Incomplete topic representations

3. **Large but incoherent topics**: Topics 17 and 18 are among the largest but have no valid POS words, suggesting they may be catch-all clusters for documents that don't fit well into other topics.

### 3.2 Implications for Downstream Analysis

**For LLM Labeling (Stage 08):**
- 13-20 topics (3.5-5.4%) are flagged for special handling
- These topics may benefit from:
  - Manual review before LLM labeling
  - Special labeling strategies (e.g., "OTHER" or "NOISE")
  - Exclusion from certain analyses

**For Model Quality:**
- 94.6-96.5% of topics have acceptable quality metrics
- The model successfully clusters most documents into interpretable topics
- Noise candidates represent a small but significant portion requiring attention

### 3.3 Limitations

1. **Threshold sensitivity**: The choice of thresholds (min_pos_words=3, min_pos_coherence=0.0) may miss subtle quality issues or flag acceptable topics.

2. **Coherence metric limitations**: The c_v coherence measure may not capture all aspects of topic quality, particularly for domain-specific content.

3. **POS representation dependency**: The analysis relies on BERTopic's POS representation aspect, which may not always capture the most semantically meaningful words.

4. **Manual review required**: The flagged topics require human judgment to determine if they represent true noise or valid but unusual topics.

The topic quality analysis successfully identified 13 candidate noisy topics (3.5% of all topics) using a combination of size, POS representation, and coherence metrics. The analysis reveals that:

1. **Most topics are high quality**: 94.6-96.5% of topics meet quality thresholds
